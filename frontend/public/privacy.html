ClockIn Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

ClockIn is a study and productivity application for tasks, notes, schedules, focus timers and progress tracking. This policy describes how the ClockIn operator handles information in this hosted application. Contact: sagi15sid05@gmail.com.

Information we process

We process your account email, username and authentication information; notes, tasks and uploaded document text; availability and schedules; timer sessions, progress rewards and onboarding preferences. We use these to provide account access, save your work, generate study plans and show your progress. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and error logs, to operate and secure the service.

Optional Google Calendar connection

If you connect Google Calendar, ClockIn requests read-only Calendar permission. The backend retrieves calendar event information and uses event titles, start and end times and dates to create saved busy-time records and avoid scheduling conflicts. The application does not create, edit or delete events in your Google Calendar. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the backend database so the connection can continue without repeated sign-in. Calendar access is optional.

ClockIn uses Google Calendar data for the availability and scheduling features you request. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Human access to Google data is restricted to consented support, necessary security or abuse investigations, or legal requirements. ClockIn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Optional AI features and personal API keys

When you request an AI feature, relevant task, note, assignment or document text is sent through our backend to the configured Groq, OpenAI or Anthropic provider. If fallback is enabled, the request may be sent to another configured provider after a failure. These providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. Avoid including sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

Personal AI keys entered in Settings are stored in this browser tab's session storage and sent with authenticated AI requests. The application does not save those personal keys in Supabase. Explicit sign-out or closing the tab clears them. Browser storage is also used for authentication, theme, onboarding and scheduling preferences.

Service providers and protection

Render hosts the frontend and API; Supabase provides authentication and database storage. Google provides the optional Calendar integration. AI providers receive content when you use their features. Information may be processed in countries where these providers operate. We use HTTPS and account access controls; no service can guarantee complete security.

Retention, disconnecting and deletion

Saved application data is retained while your account remains active. Disconnect Google Calendar in Availability to remove the stored connection tokens and imported Google busy-time records. You can also revoke ClockIn in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access at Google alone does not delete previously imported records from ClockIn.

You can delete individual tasks and notes, clear AI keys in Settings, or use Delete Account in Settings to request removal of your account and its saved application data. Provider-managed backups and operational logs may remain under those providers' retention processes. Contact us for privacy, access or deletion requests and help with records that remain after deletion.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change. Material changes to Google data use will require notice and any additional consent needed before the new use begins.
